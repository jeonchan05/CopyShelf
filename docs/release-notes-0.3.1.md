CopyShelf is a menu bar clipboard history app for text and copied images, including screenshots.

Open history with Option-Command-V by default, search, choose an item with the arrow keys, and press Enter to paste. You can change the shortcut in Settings. The choice survives restart, and a failed registration preserves the previous shortcut.

The history window keeps search and the list in focus. Settings contains background recording, optional launch at login, paste permissions, and clear history. Recording starts automatically; pause is remembered. History stays on your Mac as unencrypted JSON.

This community beta includes Apple Silicon and Intel binaries for macOS 13 or later. Intel runtime, the oldest supported macOS, and installation on other Macs remain unverified. The app is not Apple notarized; downloaded apps may need macOS's per-app Open Anyway approval. Clipboard reading and automatic paste also need their respective permissions, and updates can require permission again.

Download the DMG for a drag-to-Applications installation, or the ZIP for the same app. SHA-256 files are included. See the README for installation and privacy details.
